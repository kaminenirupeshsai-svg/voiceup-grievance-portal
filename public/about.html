About VoiceUp

An intuitive, secure platform built to collect, track, and resolve student grievances efficiently — bringing transparency to the redressal workflow for students, the grievance cell, officers, and administrators alike.

Project Overview

VoiceUp allows students to submit grievances (academic, hostel, examination, infrastructure, etc.) and lets the grievance cell triage, escalate, and resolve them with full status tracking and remarks.

Key Features

Submit Grievance

Details and attachments, timestamped and stored securely.

Role-Based Dashboards

Separate portals for students, grievance cell, officers, and admins.

Escalation Workflow

Complaints flow from cell review to officer resolution automatically.

Feedback & Ratings

Students rate how their resolution was handled.

Email Notifications

Automatic updates on status changes and new submissions.

Analytics

Trends, resolution time, and satisfaction tracked for admins.

Tech Stack

  • Backend: Node.js, Express.js
  • Frontend: EJS, HTML, CSS
  • Database: MongoDB (Mongoose ODM)
  • Authentication: Express-session + bcrypt.js
  • Notifications: Nodemailer

How It Works

1
Student submits

A grievance is filed with category, priority, and optional attachments.

2
Grievance Cell reviews

Marked in process, or forwarded to an officer if it needs escalation.

3
Officer / Admin resolves

Remarks are added and the status is updated to Resolved.

4
Student rates the outcome

A resolved complaint can be rated 1–5 stars with optional comments.
